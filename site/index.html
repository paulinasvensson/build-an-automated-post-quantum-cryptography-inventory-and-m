FAQ

How is this different from a software composition analysis (SCA) tool?

SCA tools find vulnerable dependencies; we find vulnerable cryptographic primitives buried inside code, configs, certificates, and hardware — a layer most SBOM tools never inspect.

Do we need to already know where our cryptography lives?

No — that's the point. Our scanners discover cryptographic usage across your entire estate automatically, including places security teams typically have no visibility into, like legacy services and third-party libraries.

Can this help us pass compliance audits for CNSA 2.0 or DORA?

Yes. We generate exportable, auditor-ready reports showing your current cryptographic inventory, risk exposure, and documented migration progress against regulatory timelines.

Will this replace our PQC migration consultants?

No — it makes them dramatically more effective. Instead of manual spreadsheet audits, your team or consulting partners get an automated, always-current inventory to plan and execute migration from.
